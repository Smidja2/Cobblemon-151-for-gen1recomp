Cobblemon 151 for Gen1Recomp

Adds the Cobblemon models for all 151 Gen 1 Pokémon to Gen1Recomp, along with their animations where available.

Includes followers, overworld Pokémon, ground and flying ecology, perching, random takeoff and landing, and battle models. Models are currently at 0.6 scale and toon outline can be turned on in the settings.

Not every Pokémon has the same animations in Cobblemon, so some have more than others. The mod just uses what's available for each one.

This is standalone like Rumble. Don't use it together with Rumble, Quest or Stadium since those mods also replace and control the Pokémon models and animations. Other mods should be fine as long as they don't do the same thing.

Models, textures, rigs and animations are from Cobblemon. Thanks to the Cobblemon Team and contributors for making them, and to the Gen1Recomp team and modding community.
